01 / SELECTED WORK · 2024

GOODIE

谷子消费中的情感与认同Emotion and Identity in Merchandise Consumption

概念设计 / 网站交互设计 / 三维影像
2024
1人，独立完成Solo project

《GOODIE》以二次元文化中的“吃谷”消费为背景，关注动漫周边如何承载情感陪伴、身份表达与社群认同，以及这些需求如何在直播电商和网络展示中转化为重复购买。项目借用“吃谷”与“吃播”的语言联系，将周边商品转化为虚拟食物，通过夸张的进食与堆积场景，讽刺以消费数量证明喜爱、以物质占有换取情感满足和外部认可的循环。

作品以网站为主要载体，结合三维角色、动态影像与虚拟展厅，构建可观看、互动和浏览的消费体验。吃谷吃播串联主播选择、评论互动、商品浏览与购买确认，呈现消费欲望被激发的过程；虚拟展览则提供以欣赏和探索获得满足的另一种路径。项目希望引导观众重新审视喜爱与购买的关系，在外部认同与内在满足之间寻找平衡。

GOODIE explores merchandise consumption in anime fan culture, examining how collectibles offer emotional companionship, identity expression and community belonging, and how these needs become repeated purchases through livestream commerce and online display. Drawing on the linguistic connection between “eating merch” and mukbang, the project turns merchandise into virtual food. Exaggerated scenes of eating and accumulation satirise the cycle of proving affection through consumption and seeking emotional fulfilment and external validation through ownership.

The website combines 3D characters, moving images and a virtual gallery into an experience of watching, interacting and browsing. Merchandise mukbang links host selection, comments, product browsing and purchase confirmation to reveal how desire is stimulated. The virtual exhibition offers another route to satisfaction through appreciation and exploration. The project encourages viewers to reconsider the relationship between affection and buying, seeking a balance between external recognition and inner fulfilment.

02 / SELECTED WORK · 2024

GREEN UNITY

植物的生物权利与生存伦理Plant Rights and the Ethics of Survival

思辨设计 / 三维影像 / 虚拟空间设计
2024
1人，独立完成Solo project

《GREENUNITY》从人类对植物的选择性培育与基因改造出发，关注植物在以人类需求为中心的技术体系中，被拆解、改造和工具化的处境。项目设想一座火星植物实验室：为了满足移居者对水、氧气、食物、温度调节与心理慰藉的需求，科学家将不同植物的器官重新组合，创造服务于人类生存的虚构物种。通过这一看似合理、却逐渐走向极端的实验设定，作品追问：当生存成为改造其他生命的理由，植物自身的生命价值与生物权利是否仍被尊重？

作品通过三维建模、动态影像与虚拟实验室，呈现四类改造植物及其器官结构、功能设定和培育环境。具有吸引力的色彩与材质包裹着被功能化的生命形态，使观众在欣赏这些植物的同时，意识到其背后由人类单方面制定的生存规则。项目旨在引发对人类中心主义和生命工具化的反思，倡导在追求自身生存与发展的同时，尊重、保护其他生命，重新思考人与植物之间的共生关系。

GREEN UNITY begins with selective breeding and genetic modification, examining how plants are dismantled, altered and instrumentalised within technologies centred on human needs. It imagines a botanical laboratory on Mars, where scientists recombine organs from different plants into fictional species that provide settlers with water, oxygen, food, temperature regulation and psychological comfort. Through an apparently reasonable experiment that becomes increasingly extreme, the work asks: when survival justifies altering other life forms, are the intrinsic value and biological rights of plants still respected?

3D modelling, moving images and a virtual laboratory present four types of modified plants, their organ structures, functions and growing environments. Appealing colours and materials envelop life forms reduced to functions, inviting admiration while revealing rules of survival imposed unilaterally by humans. The project questions anthropocentrism and the instrumentalisation of life, advocating respect and protection for other beings alongside human survival and development, and reconsidering the symbiotic relationship between people and plants.

03 / SELECTED WORK · 2024

LOVE SCHOOL

思辨设计 / 三维动画 / 叙事影像
2024
1人，独立完成Solo project
项目展映
BBC White City 大屏展映

《LOVESCHOOL》是一部以虚构的爱情学校宣传片为形式的三维影片。作品设想一个失去爱人能力的未来社会，以斯滕伯格“爱情三角理论”中的亲密、激情与承诺为框架，将情感拆解成可以教授、模仿与考核的课程。

影片从学校介绍开始，依次展示三门课程，最终由学校颁发毕业证书，授予学生结婚资格。作品借此探讨爱情被标准化的过程，以及人们在建立真实关系与表演符合社会期待的爱情之间的矛盾。

LOVE SCHOOL is a 3D film presented as a promotional video for a fictional school of love. It imagines a future society that has lost the ability to love. Using intimacy, passion and commitment from Sternberg’s triangular theory of love as its framework, the film breaks emotions down into courses that can be taught, imitated and assessed.

The film introduces the school, presents its three courses and concludes with the award of graduation certificates granting students eligibility to marry. It explores the standardisation of love and the tension between forming authentic relationships and performing love that meets social expectations.

05 / SELECTED WORK · 2025–2026

SHAPED EMOTION

交互装置 / 三维影像 / 思辨设计
2025–2026
3人；本人负责研究、叙事与视觉设计Team of 3 / Research, narrative & visual design
项目展映
BBC White City 大屏展映 / RCA 毕业展

《SHAPEDEMOTION》以中国职场中的情绪管理要求为背景，结合问卷、访谈与理论研究，关注“情绪稳定”“高情商”等社会期待如何进入日常生活，并逐渐被内化为个人习惯。项目通过主人公的经历，呈现一个从接受外部规训，到无意识地观察、修正和约束自身情绪的过程。

作品结合三维影片、实时面部交互与实体情绪商品，以空间秩序、日常标语、声音和重复行为暗示这一转变，让观众在观看中逐步体会。交互装置则邀请观众根据系统反馈调整表情，使其在尝试符合要求的过程中，也成为规训的一部分。影片进一步将自我调整与情绪消费连接起来，呈现外部标准如何以个人选择的形式不断延续。

SHAPED EMOTION examines emotional management in Chinese workplaces. Drawing on questionnaires, interviews and theoretical research, it explores how expectations such as “emotional stability” and “high emotional intelligence” enter daily life and gradually become personal habits. Through its protagonist, the project traces a shift from accepting external discipline to unconsciously monitoring, correcting and restraining one’s own emotions.

The work combines a 3D film, real-time facial interaction and physical emotional commodities. Spatial order, everyday slogans, sound and repetitive behaviour subtly convey this transformation, allowing viewers to sense it gradually. The installation invites participants to adjust their expressions in response to system feedback, becoming part of the disciplinary process as they try to meet its demands. The film further connects self-adjustment with emotional consumption, revealing how external standards persist in the guise of personal choice.

04 / SELECTED WORK · 2025

TONGUE ATLAS

身体信息图谱Reading the Body

书籍设计 / 视觉实验 / 交互实验
2025
独立项目 / Solo project

《Tongue Atlas》是一项结合书籍设计、信息可视化与交互体验的项目，探索传统舌象知识在数字语境下的视觉表达。针对相关知识专业性强、普通读者难以理解的问题，项目将舌头视为一张记录身体线索的“地图”，通过图像分类与三维形态实验，将舌色、舌苔和纹理转化为直观的视觉信息。

书籍涵盖舌面分区、健康舌象、四类典型案例，以及舌色与舌苔的分类图谱，并结合舌象扫描交互原型，连接阅读与观察，引导使用者关注自身的身体变化。

Tongue Atlas combines book design, information visualisation and interactive experiences to explore how traditional knowledge of tongue appearance can be expressed visually in a digital context. To make this specialised knowledge more accessible to general readers, the project treats the tongue as a “map” of bodily clues. Through image classification and experiments with three-dimensional forms, it translates tongue colour, coating and texture into intuitive visual information.

The book covers tongue surface regions, healthy tongue appearances, four typical case categories, and classified atlases of tongue colours and coatings. Paired with an interactive tongue-scanning prototype, it connects reading with observation and encourages users to notice changes in their own bodies.

项目属于视觉与交互设计探索，不作为临床诊断工具。
An exploration of visual and interaction design, rather than a clinical diagnostic tool.
